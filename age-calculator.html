How to use the Age Calculator

Choose your date of birth and the date you want to calculate your age on. Press “Calculate Age” to get your exact age in completed years, months and days. The calculator also shows the number of days until your next birthday.

What does exact age mean?

Exact age is the time elapsed between your birth date and the selected calculation date. The result accounts for different month lengths and leap years.

Is this age calculator free?

Yes. Taraazu’s age calculator is free to use on mobile phones, tablets and computers. Your dates are processed in your browser and are not sent to a server.
